        About Hasbuk

        Quran teachers test students by opening the mushaf at a random place and asking them to continue until they say hasbuk (حسبك, "enough"). Pick a range, tap Random ayah, and recite on from there.

        Play with friends

        Everyone gets the same ayahs in the same order, shown the same way, and plays when they like. Each person who plays adds their score and can send it on.

        Play together now, in the same place or on a call. You pick each ayah and everyone sees it at once. Friends join with the code or QR code.
